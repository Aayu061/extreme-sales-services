1. Service Booking & Dispatch

When you book an AC inspection, cleaning, or repair service:

  • A technician is dispatched based on location proximity and priority level.
  • Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) is calculated based on current traffic and active technician queue.
  • The technician conducts an initial inspection and provides an upfront quote before starting chargeable part replacements.

2. Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

AMC subscriptions cover routine wet washes, electrical checks, filter cleaning, and priority breakdown attendance as specified in the chosen tier (Basic, Standard, Comprehensive).

Compressor replacement or copper piping extension costs apply separately unless explicitly included in the Comprehensive plan agreement.

3. Genuine Spare Parts & Warranty

All replacement spare parts (capacitors, fan motors, PCB boards, thermistors, expansion valves) installed by Extreme Sales & Services carry a 90-day parts warranty.

4. Payment Terms

Payments can be made via UPI, cash, or digital payment upon successful completion of work and testing in the presence of the customer. Invoices are dispatched digitally.

5. Customer Support & Dispute Resolution

If you are not satisfied with cooling performance post-servicing, our escalation team will arrange a free re-visit within 7 days of the completed job.

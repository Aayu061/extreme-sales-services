Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you book an AC service, enquire about certified products, or subscribe to an AMC contract with Extreme Sales & Services, we collect necessary contact information:

  • Full Name and Contact Number (for engineer dispatch and OTP verification).
  • Service Location / Doorstep Address (for technician on-site routing).
  • AC Make, Model, and Reported Issue details.
  • Digital signature captured at service completion for invoice verification.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your details are strictly used to fulfill doorstep AC services, provide real-time tracking, generate warranty certificates, and deliver service updates via SMS or WhatsApp.

We do not sell, lease, or monetize customer phone numbers or addresses to third-party telemarketers.

3. Data Security & Retention

All service requests, tracking statuses, and customer records are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.

4. Cookies & Local Preferences

We use lightweight client-side storage (localStorage) solely to save your dark/light theme choice and keep your session authenticated across browser visits.

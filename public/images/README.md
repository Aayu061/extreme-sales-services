# Product & Spare Parts Image Directory

This directory stores offline/local assets for AC systems and spare parts.

## Directory Structure
- `public/images/products/` — Photos of Split ACs, Window ACs, Inverter units, Commercial HVAC.
- `public/images/spares/` — Photos of genuine spare parts (capacitors, fan motors, copper piping, compressors, PCB boards, sensors, gas cans).

## 2 Ways to Add Images from Your PC

### Method 1: Upload Directly from the Admin Portal (Recommended)
1. Go to the Admin Dashboard -> **Products & Spares** tab.
2. Click **+ Add New Product**.
3. Under **Product Image**, make sure **From PC** is selected.
4. Click the dashed box to choose any image from your computer (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`), or drag and drop it.
5. You'll see a live thumbnail preview.
6. Click **Save Product** — the image is automatically optimized and saved to your catalog and public marketplace!

### Method 2: Manual Folder Copy
1. Copy your `.jpg` or `.png` image files directly into:
   `Extreme-Sales-Services-main/public/images/products/`
   or
   `Extreme-Sales-Services-main/public/images/spares/`
2. In the Admin modal, switch to **Image URL** and type:
   `/images/products/your-image-name.jpg`
3. Click **Save Product**.
